# data/vocab_en_core.txt

17,708 strings: common English words in lower case and Title case (+ a few archaic forms such as 'Tis, Thy, Ye and
Mario names). Derived from the Hunspell en_US dictionary (SCOWL, (c) Kevin Atkinson and contributors, permissive
licence — see http://wordlist.aspell.net/scowl-readme/) with light affix expansion, filtered to words that occur at
least 20 times in a local text corpus (to drop rare words). Used as the vocabulary of the line-2 dictionary search and
the word-2 test.
